# Google OAuth production check

On 2026-10-28 the owner authorized Google OAuth after their account was added to the matching Google Cloud project's External/Testing test-user list. Fluxora returned to Connections and showed a managed Google OAuth credential. Using temporary unsaved editor nodes, the Google Sheets picker loaded 20 spreadsheets and the Google Drive text-file picker loaded 100 files. No file content was opened or modified, no node was saved or published, and no secret was recorded. This does not establish refresh behavior at expiry or action write semantics.

Testing-mode consent and offline refresh tokens for Drive/Sheets can expire after seven days ([Google Cloud Audience](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549945?hl=en)). The current controller requests `drive` (restricted) and `spreadsheets` (sensitive). Google recommends considering `drive.file` and Google Picker for per-file access; changing scopes alone would change the current all-files listing behavior and may require reauthorization ([Drive scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/api-specific-auth), [Sheets scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/scopes)). Review verification, server-side data handling, picker flow and migration before any scope change. Google Cloud remains External/Testing with publishing disabled pending branding configuration.
